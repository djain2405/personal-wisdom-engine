---
title: Identity before outcomes
source: personal reflection
date: 2026-07-01
tags: [identity, discipline]
---

The person I am becoming decides what I do today—not my mood, not my inbox.

When I act from the identity of someone who keeps promises to themselves, discipline stops feeling like force and starts feeling like alignment.

Recurring idea: protect one keystone habit every morning before reactive work. That habit is evidence of identity.

Questions I return to:
- Who am I being right now?
- What would the future version of me do in the next hour?
- Which small action proves the identity I claim?
