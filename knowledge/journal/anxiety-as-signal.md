When anxiety spikes, I often try to think my way out. What works better:

1. Name it: "This is fear of uncertainty."
2. Body first: breathe, walk, drink water.
3. One next action from my principles—not from the spiral.

Principle: feelings are information; identity chooses the response.

Reflection: Where did I let anxiety choose for me today, and where did I choose back?
