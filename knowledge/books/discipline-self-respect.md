Discipline is not punishment. It is self-respect expressed as consistent action.

Patterns noticed across sources:
1. Start smaller than ego wants.
2. Make the environment do half the work.
3. Track streaks as identity evidence, not moral scorekeeping.

Mental model: "Never miss twice." Missing once is life. Missing twice is a new pattern.

Actionable habit: decide the night before the first 10-minute move of the morning.
